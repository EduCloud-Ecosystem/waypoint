(-1, 0, 1). These examples and the two expected results were written from that
literal arithmetic, not generated by a workload implementation.

Baseline keeps eight values with total 110 and mean 13.75. Alternative removes
one 1 and one 100 from `001`, retaining six values with total 9 and mean 1.5.
It leaves the three-value `NA` group unchanged. All values are synthetic.
